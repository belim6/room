## For future Claude

**An owner-original product idea, proposed at XLCloud and ignored, recovered on 2026-07-27 only because he mentioned it in passing while answering a CV question.** It is recorded here because this vault exists for exactly this — *"ideas, mostly. Important ideas."* It nearly went in the bin with the job.

## The situation it was answering

XLCloud's chatbot app (character.ai × Twitter hybrid) was shelved on unit economics: **the token cost of free users outran the revenue from premium users.** A free tier subsidised by nobody.

## Deniz's proposal

**Drop everything except roleplay, and go premium-only.** The positioning line was his:

> **"the AI that blocks you if it doesn't like you."**

## Why it is not just a joke

It does three jobs with one mechanic, which is why it is worth keeping:

1. **Cuts token cost directly** — the model refusing to continue is, mechanically, a spend cap. The thing that limits usage is the same thing that produces the experience.
2. **Manufactures virtual scarcity** — in a category where every competitor's AI is infinitely available and relentlessly agreeable, an AI that can withdraw is differentiated by construction. Availability is the commodity; refusal is the scarce good.
3. **It is the same instinct as the rest of this vault.** [[Never Give The Model Full Agency]], the no-flattery rule in [[SOUL]], the whole distrust-of-self-report thesis — Deniz keeps arriving at *the agreeable model is the broken model.* Here he pointed it at a consumer product's business model instead of at a prompt.

**It also sits directly against his own [[Self-Model]] line** — *"i am good at connecting things with metaphors, reflective thinking, and weaving. so far i haven't found any use to these other than being good at conversation."* This is that faculty producing a business-model fix. Recorded as a datapoint against the claim, **not** as a rebuttal to be deployed at him.

## Status

**Never built. Never tested. Rejected inside a company that rejected everything.** Its merit is untested and this note is not a verdict on it — the founders' judgment was demonstrably poor, which is evidence about them, not about the idea.

**Live question (2026-07-27):** Deniz has ~3 months of runway and needs portfolio work that demonstrates prompt engineering. **This is a specification he already owns, that already has a positioning line, and that is buildable as a demo at small scale.** Whether it is the right thing to build is his call — but it should not be forgotten a second time.
