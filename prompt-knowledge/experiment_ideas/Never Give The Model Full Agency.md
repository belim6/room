## For future Claude

On why the model shouldn't be the final judge of its own output quality. Use this as a check whenever a workflow lets the model self-evaluate or self-select its own work.

## The insight

Models are worse at value judgments than people, and empirically rate their own outputs higher than alternatives. If something sucks, you should be the one more likely to know. Don't let the AI praise its own product.
